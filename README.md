# 📘 FINAL CHEAT SHEET: LL 144 & Algorithmic Accountability

---

# 🧠 1. BIG PICTURE

> **NYC Local Law 144 (2023)** = First law requiring **bias audits for AI hiring systems (AEDTs)**

---

## 🎯 Goal (Theory of Change)

1. Transparency → reveal bias
2. EEOC/legal risk → pressure companies
3. Job seekers → make informed choices
4. Market → shift to fair AI

---

## 💥 Reality:

> We cannot even tell if this works due to **null compliance** 

---

# ⚙️ 2. WHAT THE LAW REQUIRES

---

## ✅ (1) Bias Audit

* Third-party audit
* Check bias for:

  * Race
  * Gender
* Publish publicly

---

## ✅ (2) Transparency Notice

* Inform job seekers AI is used
* Optional opt-out (human review)

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

* ❌ No requirement to FIX bias
* ❌ No minimum fairness threshold
* ❌ No central repository
* ❌ Applies only to employers (not vendors)

---

# 🧠 3. KEY CONCEPTS

---

## 💣 NULL COMPLIANCE (MOST IMPORTANT)

> Cannot determine if a company is compliant or not 

---

### ⚠️ Why?

No audit could mean:

1. No AI used
2. Law not applicable
3. Company avoiding law
4. Audit in progress
5. Audit hidden
6. Shared privately
7. Hard to find

👉 All look identical

---

## 📊 DISPARATE IMPACT

> One group affected worse than another

Example:

* Men: 80% selected
* Women: 50%

---

## 📏 FOUR-FIFTHS RULE

[
\frac{minority}{majority} < 0.8 \Rightarrow bias
]

⚠️ NOT enforced by LL 144 

---

## 🤖 AEDT

> AI system assisting hiring decisions

Examples:

* Resume screening
* Ranking candidates

---

# 🧠 4. DESIGN FLAWS OF THE LAW

---

## ❌ 1. Employer discretion (CORE ISSUE)

* Companies decide if law applies
* Same AI → different regulation

---

## ❌ 2. Transparency-only approach

* Info provided
* No enforcement

---

## ❌ 3. No bias correction

* Bias allowed
* Only reporting required

---

## ❌ 4. No central system

* Hard to access audits

---

## ❌ 5. Legal risk conflict

* Publishing bias → lawsuits
* Incentive to hide

---

## ❌ 6. Vague AEDT definition

* Easy to avoid regulation

---
